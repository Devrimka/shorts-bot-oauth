DevrimKa Shorts Bot

This is a personal, single-user automation project that generates short historical narration videos (YouTube Shorts) and publishes them to the DevrimKa YouTube channel.

The pipeline writes a short factual narration about a historical event, synthesizes voice narration for it, composes a background video from public-domain archival images, and uploads the finished video to YouTube using the YouTube Data API (OAuth2) under the channel owner's own account.

This application is used by its owner only. It does not collect, store, or process any data belonging to other people, and it does not act on behalf of any account other than the owner's own YouTube channel.

See the Privacy Policy for details on data use.
